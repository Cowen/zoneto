## Changes Made

### 1. Remove `postal_fsa` from `DEV_CAT_COLS` (features.py)

**Why:** With only 69 minority-class examples and 300+ unique FSAs in Toronto,
the feature caused AUC variance to explode from ±0.177 to ±0.267 — clear evidence
of fold-specific memorization, not generalization.

### 2. Remove ward profiles from `COA_NUM_COLS` (features.py)

**Why:** `ward_number` is already a categorical feature in `COA_CAT_COLS`, making
continuous ward demographics redundant. COA decisions are made on individual site
conditions by panels, not neighbourhood demographics. No measurable improvement
was seen (AUC 0.534 unchanged).

**Note:** Ward profiles are retained in `DEV_NUM_COLS` where demographic context
is more plausible for development application decisions.

### 3. Remove `application_year` from `PERMIT_NUM_COLS` (features.py)

**Why:** `application_year` caused R² to regress from 0.042 to 0.008. The feature
captures COVID-era queue depth patterns that reversed sign across temporal splits
(high year → long processing in training; high year → shorter processing in test).
Removing it recovers ~0.034 R².

### 4. Retire `dev_applications_approved` from `train_all()` (train.py)

**Why:** Dataset is frozen (city retired it, no new records), 97.3% class imbalance
(69 refusals in 2,515 rows), AUC variance ±0.267 (worst fold ≈ 0.453, worse than
random). Model was already retired from `score_all` in a prior session.
